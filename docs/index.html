Rules in one minute

8×8 board, two fog banks. Each side commits 16 pieces as salted hashes: everyone sees where your pieces are, not what they are. Every move is a transaction. In a clash both ranks are revealed and checked against the commitments. Capture the enemy Beacon to win. If you stall past your turn timer, you forfeit.

Higher rank wins, and ties go to the defender. The Ghost ambushes the Warden when it attacks. Mines are single use, and Sappers defuse them. Scouts move up to 3 squares. The winner must prove a legal army before it collects.

Fogline is an original, open-source TapeLog project. Testnet only: stakes use a valueless test token and real-money wagering is disabled. Cosmetics are decorative only. Game for entertainment. Not investment advice.
