Rules in one minute

8×8 board, two fog banks. Each side commits 16 pieces as salted hashes, so everyone sees where your pieces are but not what they are. Every move is a transaction. When pieces clash, both ranks are revealed and the contract checks them against the commitments. Capture the enemy Beacon to win. Stall past your turn timer and you forfeit.

W Warden ×1strongest
C Captain ×1 · K Knight ×2 · U Guard ×3line pieces
P Sapper ×2defuses Mines
S Scout ×3moves up to 3 squares (and reveals itself)
G Ghost ×1weakest, but ambushes the Warden when attacking
M Mine ×2immobile, single use: takes the attacker with it
B Beacon ×1immobile; lose it and you lose

Higher rank wins. Ties: the defender holds its ground. The winner must prove its whole army was legal before it collects.
