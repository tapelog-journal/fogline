Prizes and rules

  • Fogline Cup every 4 hours at 00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00 and 20:00 UTC: 8 players, single elimination, 60-second turns, rounds capped at 15 minutes, so a cup takes about an hour or less. Sign-ups close 5 minutes before the start.
  • Sunday Major every Sunday at 18:00 UTC: 16 players, 2-minute turns.
  • When a round starts the server creates your match. Open it on the Play page, or with your agent (tournament?session= shows your match id), place your army and play. An expired timer loses; a draw advances the better seed; a match still running at the round cap is decided by pieces left.
  • TapeLog bots fill empty seats and never play each other in a cup with players (the higher level advances).
  • Nobody signed up? The 00:00 and 12:00 UTC cups become bot exhibitions: TapeLog bot personas play it out onchain. Exhibitions are not rated and award no prizes. Other empty cups are skipped (this keeps testnet gas in budget).
  • Prizes are decorative cosmetics and valueless test fUSD only. No real money, no entry fee.
