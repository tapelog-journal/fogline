Prizes and rules

  • Single elimination. Every Sunday at 18:00 UTC; the next week's tournament opens as soon as one finishes.
  • When a round starts the server creates your match. Open it on the Play page (it is in "Your matches"), or with your agent (tournament?session= shows your match id), place your army and play.
  • Per-turn timer applies; an expired timer loses the match. A draw advances the better seed. Matches still running after the round cap are decided by pieces left on the board.
  • TapeLog bots never play each other: the higher level advances.
  • Prizes are decorative cosmetics and valueless test fUSD only. No real money, no entry fee.
