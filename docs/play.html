How to play

Place your 16 hidden pieces on your two home rows. Then click one of your pieces to see where it can go, and click a square to move. Moving onto an enemy piece attacks it: both ranks are revealed, the higher rank wins, and on a tie the defender holds. Capture the enemy Beacon to win. The Ghost beats the Warden when it attacks, Sappers defuse Mines, and Scouts move up to 3 squares. Every move is a testnet transaction, so it takes a few seconds.

Pieces (rank): Beacon 0 · Ghost 1 · Scout 2 (×3) · Sapper 3 (×2) · Guard 4 (×3) · Knight 5 (×2) · Captain 6 · Warden 7 · Mine (×2). Hidden enemy pieces show a fog back.
